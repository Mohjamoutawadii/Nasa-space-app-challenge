WHY EXPLORE THIS REGION?

Three Reasons to Go South

☀️

Extreme Sunlight

Some elevated areas near the South Pole can receive sunlight for long periods. This could help future explorers generate solar energy.

ENERGY POTENTIAL
🌑

Permanent Shadows

Some deep craters never receive direct sunlight. These permanently shadowed regions are among the coldest places on the Moon.

EXTREME ENVIRONMENT
💧

Possible Water Ice

Scientists have found evidence of water ice and other frozen materials in some lunar polar regions. More exploration is needed.
